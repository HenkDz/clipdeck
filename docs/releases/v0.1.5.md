The popup can be moved and dismissed from every view.

- Drag Updates, Settings and preview windows by their header; use the chip-row
  grip in the clip list. Dragging also works with Keep open turned off.
- Every view has Close and Keep open on top controls. Turning Keep open off
  removes the topmost state immediately.
- Reopening an already visible popup preserves its position and current view.
- Updating saves the current window bounds and restores them after restart.
- Includes signed updates and paste counts beside centered icons.

v0.1.1 and earlier need one manual installation. History and settings stay in
`%APPDATA%\Clipdeck`; no database migration. Updates require a writable app folder.
Update metadata is signed; the executable has no Authenticode certificate.
